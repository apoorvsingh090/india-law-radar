The Sample Act, 2026

An Act to provide a deterministic fixture for testing ingestion.

Section 1 — Short title and commencement

This Act may be called the Sample Act, 2026. It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may appoint.

Section 2 — Definitions

In this Act, "fixture" means a labelled test document that carries no legal force.

Section 3 — Compliance threshold

Every establishment shall file the quarterly return within thirty days of the end of the quarter.
